FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions below.

Open Tasks and click "Add Task", or use the floating "+" button on Home, Calendar, Tasks, Grocery, or Pill Reminders. Enter a title, choose a category (Personal, Family, Household, or Pill Reminder), select the date and time, and click "Create Task".
On Pill Reminders, add a medication name, optional dosage, date, and intake time. Each reminder is scheduled once for the chosen date and time. Mark it as taken after the medication is taken.
Enter an item and a whole-number quantity, then click "ADD". Mark it purchased to move it to Recently Purchased. Your recent purchases also appear in Recommended so you can add them again.
You can easily toggle between Light Mode and Dark Mode by clicking the moon/sun icon in the top header or through Profile > Settings > Theme Mode. Your preference is automatically saved.
Check the message beside the form and correct any highlighted fields. If your session has expired, sign in again. For connection errors, wait until the connection is available and try again. The Report a Problem page lets you check report details; it does not send or save reports.
